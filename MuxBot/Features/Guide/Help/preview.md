# Live preview

A bot with a website can show it beside the chat. darkmux runs the site from its work copy, so you see each run's changes.

## Turning it on

darkmux needs a preview recipe that says how the project starts. When there isn't one, choose Set up preview: Mux Bot works it out from the project's files on a card for you to check and approve. Without one, you can add a preview link in the bot's info.

## Pointing at things

Use Pick in the preview and click an element, or select text and choose Chat about this. It goes into the chat bar, and what you type decides what happens: ask for a change ("make this bigger", "replace this with…") and the bot proposes the work for you to approve; describe a problem ("why is this cut off") and it measures the element and drafts a bug; ask a question ("what font is this") and it answers. Bots can't see the page, so they measure it instead.

## Editing text yourself

On the Mac, select text in the preview and choose Edit. Type the new words and choose Save. Mux Bot changes the one place in your project that holds that text and saves it as a change you can undo from the thread. If the text isn't in your files exactly once (it's built by code, split by formatting, or repeated), the bot offers the change to Coder instead, for you to approve.

## Using your own pictures

On the Mac, you can bring in your own photos and logos (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB). To swap a picture on the page, use Pick, click the picture, and choose Replace picture in the chat bar, then choose a file. Mux Bot puts your picture in the project and shows it in that spot, as a change you can undo from the thread. If it can't find that spot in your files, your picture is still added and Coder is offered the change, for you to approve. To add a picture somewhere new, choose the picture button next to the chat bar and pick a file. The bar then names your picture, so you can finish the message by saying where it goes, like "as the background of the top section".
