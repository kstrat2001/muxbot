# Bots

A bot is a project or theme you name, like your website or an app. Each bot has its own thread, its resources, and its runs.

## Making a bot

Use New bot (+), give it a name and a face, then say what it's about and add its code: a project folder you already have (on the Mac) or a GitHub link. Or ask Mux Bot to make one from a repo link.

## Resources

A bot keeps its GitHub repo, a preview link, other links and notes. Add more from the bot's info.

## Work copies

When you add a folder, darkmux makes its own copy of the project on a new branch, so your files and changes are never touched. With only a GitHub link, darkmux clones the repo the first time a run needs it.

## Hiding a bot

Hide from Sidebar takes a bot out of the sidebar and Home without stopping anything: its runs and preview go on. Hidden bots are under Hidden Bots at the bottom of the sidebar (on iPhone, More on Home), and a hidden bot shows up top again while something waits for you. Show in Sidebar puts it back.

## Archiving a bot

Done with a bot for now? Choose Archive from its menu (right-click on the Mac, touch and hold on iPhone and iPad). It leaves your bot list, but its conversation, runs and links are kept on all your devices. A bot with a run going, or something waiting for you, can be archived once that's finished.

To bring one back, open Archived (in the sidebar, or More on Home) and choose Restore. Deleting a bot for good is there too, and it can't be undone.

## Clearing a conversation

Clear Conversation empties a bot's thread; its runs stay in Activity. Right after, the thread offers Undo, which brings the messages back until Mux Bot quits.

## The crew

Under each bot is darkmux's crew: Coder, Reviewer, Eval runner and Researcher. A bot sends work to the crew member that fits; you see who on the dispatch card.
