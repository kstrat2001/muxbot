# Bots

A bot is a project or theme you name, like your website or an app. Each bot has its own thread, its resources, and its runs.

## Making a bot

Choose New (+): start a project from a template, or Work on a project I already have for a folder on this Mac or a repo on your GitHub. For a bot with no project, choose Just a bot, no project at the bottom, give it a name and a face, and say what it's about. Or ask Mux Bot to make one from a repo link.

## Resources

A bot keeps its GitHub repo, a preview link, other links and notes. Add more from the bot's info.

## Work copies

When you add a folder, darkmux makes its own copy of the project on a new branch, so your files and changes are never touched. With only a GitHub link, darkmux clones the repo the first time a run needs it.

## Hiding a bot

Hide from Sidebar takes a bot out of the sidebar and Home without stopping anything: its runs and preview go on. Hidden bots are under Hidden Bots at the bottom of the sidebar (on iPhone, the Hidden and Archived menu on Home), and a hidden bot shows up top again while something waits for you. Show in Sidebar puts it back.

## Archiving a bot

Done with a bot for now? Choose Archive from its menu (right-click on the Mac, touch and hold on iPhone and iPad). It leaves your bot list, but its conversation, runs and links are kept on all your devices. A bot with a run going can be archived once the run finishes. Anything waiting for you comes back when you restore the bot.

To bring one back, open Archived (in the sidebar, or the Hidden and Archived menu on Home) and choose Restore. Deleting a bot for good is there too, and it can't be undone.

## Clearing a conversation

Clear Conversation empties a bot's thread; its runs stay in Activity. Right after, the thread offers Undo, which brings the messages back until Mux Bot quits.

## The crew

Under each bot is darkmux's crew: Coder, Reviewer, Eval runner and Researcher. A bot sends work to the crew member that fits; you see who on the dispatch card.
