# What you can ask for

A bot works on one project, like your website. You tell it what you want in your own words, the way you'd ask a person who builds websites.

## Things to ask for

- Change words: "Change the headline to Fresh bread every morning".
- Change the look: "Make it dark green", "use a bigger font for the titles", "more space between sections".
- Add or remove parts: "Add an About page", "add a section with three services", "remove the contact part".
- Fix how it looks on a phone: "The menu is squished on my phone".
- Point at something: tap Point at something, click the part of the page you mean, then say what to change, like "make this bigger".

Your bot's skills show up as buttons above the chat bar. Tap one and the bot asks what exactly you want.

## How a change happens

The bot writes the change as a card. Nothing changes until you tap Approve. When it's done, the preview shows the result. Reply to adjust it ("a little lighter"), or say "undo" to take it back.

## What the bot can't do

The bot can't see your page the way you do. When something looks wrong, point at it or describe it, and the bot measures the page to find what's off. It doesn't put your site on the internet: Start review, then Merge, saves your changes to the project on GitHub.

## When you're not sure

Say "help me" or "what can I do?" in a bot's thread. The bot says where your project stands and offers the next things to try.
