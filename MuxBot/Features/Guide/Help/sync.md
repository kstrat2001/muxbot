# Sync

With iCloud sync on, your bots, runs and threads sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. Settings › Models syncs too.

## What stays on one device

Your darkmux pairing, your GitHub sign-in, picked project folders and darkmux work copies stay on the device where you set them up.

## Turning it on

Sync is part of how the app is built. When it's off, everything stays on this device.
