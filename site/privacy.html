Mux Bot

Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

Mux Bot is built so that your work stays with you. The people who make Mux Bot do not run a server for the app and do not receive your projects, your conversations or any information about how you use it.

What stays on your devices

  • Your bots, conversations and runs are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they sync between your own devices through your private iCloud storage, which Apple runs and we cannot read.
  • Your project files live on your Mac. The AI models that work on them run on your Mac through darkmux.
  • Sign-in details for GitHub and for your Mac's darkmux are kept in the Keychain on your device.

What leaves your devices, and when

  • GitHub. The app reads its list of templates from GitHub. If you sign in with GitHub, the app sends your project's files to repositories in your own GitHub account when you back a project up or open a pull request. GitHub's own privacy statement covers what GitHub does with that.
  • Live previews. A preview shows the site you are building. If your site loads something from the internet, that request goes to whoever serves it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No third-party code in the app: it uses Apple's own frameworks only.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its headline font from Google Fonts. Both receive your IP address when you visit, as any web host does. The site sets no cookies.

Questions

Mux Bot is open source, so you can check any of this in the code. To ask a question, open an issue on GitHub.
