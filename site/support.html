Mux Bot

Support

Help with Mux Bot, the app for building websites and books with AI that runs on your Mac.

Start in the app

Mux Bot, the octopus on the Home screen, can check your setup and answer questions. Ask him "check my setup" and he will show what is ready and what still needs doing, with a button or a guide for each step.

Setting up darkmux

Mux Bot needs darkmux on an Apple silicon Mac to run its models. The guide for installing and setting it up is at darkmux.com.

Report a problem or ask a question

Mux Bot is open source. To report a bug, ask a question or suggest something, open an issue on GitHub. Please say which device you are on and what you were doing when it went wrong.
